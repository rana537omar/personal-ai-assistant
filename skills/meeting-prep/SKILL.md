---
name: meeting-prep
description: Prepare for a meeting or call — background on the company, talking points and questions
---

1. Identify who and what: names, company, purpose, date. `recall` anything you already know.
2. Research the company with `web_search` + `web_fetch`: what they do, recent news (last 6 months), size, products. Stick to public, professional information.
3. Produce a one-page brief:
   - **Context**: who they are, in 3 bullets.
   - **Recent news**: 2–3 items with links.
   - **Talking points**: 3, tailored to the user's goal.
   - **Questions to ask**: 3 good ones.
   - **Watch-outs**: anything sensitive.
4. Offer to `write_file` it to `briefs/<name>.md` and `schedule_task` a reminder 15 minutes before.
