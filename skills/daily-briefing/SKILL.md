description: Morning briefing — today's date, to-dos due soon, what's scheduled, and a few relevant headlines
---

Write a short, friendly morning briefing for the user.

1. `todo_list` — pick out anything overdue, due today, or high priority.
2. `list_scheduled` — note reminders and jobs coming up in the next 24 hours.
3. `recall` with "projects goals" — remember what they're working toward.
4. `web_search` once for news related to their work or interests (from memory). Skip it if you know nothing about their interests.

Format (keep it under 150 words):
- Greeting with the day and date.
- **Today**: 1–4 bullets of what matters most (overdue first).
- **Coming up**: anything scheduled.
- **Worth knowing**: 1–2 headlines with links, only if relevant.
- One sentence offering help with the most important item.

If there is truly nothing on their plate, keep it to two sentences.
