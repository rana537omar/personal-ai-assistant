---
name: weekly-review
description: Weekly review — what got done, what slipped, and a focused plan for next week
---

1. `todo_list` with include_done true — split into done this week, still open, and overdue.
2. `recall` "goals projects" — what they're working toward.
3. `list_scheduled` — what's already booked for next week.

Write the review:
- **Wins**: what got done (be specific and encouraging).
- **Slipped**: open or overdue items — for each, suggest do, delegate, reschedule or drop.
- **Next week's top 3**: the three things that move their goals most.
- **One question** to help them reflect.

Ask before changing anything; then use `todo_add`, `todo_done` and `schedule_task` as agreed.
