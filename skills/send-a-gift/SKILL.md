---
name: send-a-gift
description: Help send flowers or a gift to someone — ideas, delivery options, and a card message
---

1. Who and why: `recall` the person's name (relationship, likes, city, important dates). Ask for the occasion, budget and delivery date if unknown.
2. Ideas: suggest 3 options that fit what you know about the recipient (flowers, a book in their genre, food they love…), each with a rough price.
3. Delivery: `web_search` for services that deliver to their city by the date; check one or two with `web_fetch`. Give direct product links.
4. Draft a short card message in the user's voice (2–3 lines), plus a more playful alternative.
5. **Never order or pay** — share the links and let the user check out.
6. Save useful facts with `remember` (e.g. "Sara loves white tulips") and offer a `schedule_task` reminder before the next occasion.
