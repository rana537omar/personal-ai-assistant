---
name: web-research
description: Research a question across several sources and answer with citations
---

Use this for questions that need current or detailed information from the web.

1. Turn the question into 1–2 focused `web_search` queries (add the year for anything time-sensitive).
2. Pick the 2–3 most authoritative results (official sites, reputable publications) and read them with `web_fetch`.
3. If sources disagree, say so and prefer the most official and most recent one.
4. Answer directly first (1–3 sentences), then the key details as bullets.
5. End with sources as links: `[Title](url)`.

Never invent numbers, dates, prices or quotes — only report what a fetched page actually says. If you couldn't verify something, say so.
